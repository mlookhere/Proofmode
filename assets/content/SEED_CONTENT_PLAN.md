# First 100 Posts — Seed Content Plan

Do not launch ProofTV empty. The first feed should teach the culture before users create it themselves.

## Required mix
- 20 Proof/win posts
- 20 Fail/chaos posts
- 15 Comeback/reset posts
- 10 First Day posts
- 10 PR/milestone posts
- 10 Rivalry/leader-change posts
- 5 Crew moments
- 5 creator-host posts
- 5 explainer/onboarding posts

## Vertical mix
- 25 fitness/run/gym
- 15 funny/everyday
- 15 food/cooking
- 15 build/business
- 10 study/learning
- 10 art/creative
- 10 outdoors/social

## Story pairs to pre-produce
1. Failed 275 bench → next-day redemption.
2. Burned dinner → completed meal → friend remixes challenge.
3. Missed 5AM twice → reset → seven-day comeback.
4. First mile in years → day 7 → friend joins.
5. Creator opens Drop → follower takes #1 → creator responds.
6. Crew falling behind → group rescue → team overtakes rival.
7. Bad sketch → day 14 improvement comparison.
8. Sales-call fear → first call → 20-call finish.
9. Rain run hesitation → post anyway → reaction wave.
10. “Touch Grass” joke post → group-chat chain.

## Content quality rule
Every post should satisfy at least two of:
- funny
- impressive
- relatable
- suspenseful
- useful
- emotionally human
- easy to copy

And every post must expose at least one participation doorway:
- Try this
- Join Drop
- Run it back
- Beat them
- Follow Journey
- Keep chain alive
- Challenge friend

## Creator seeding rule
A creator's first Drop should have at least 8–15 real posts/members before they push it publicly whenever possible. Empty rooms destroy social proof.
