# ProofMode — KPI & Analytics Plan

## North Star — Weekly Meaningful Participants (WMP)
Rolling-7-day unique users who do at least one:
- publish a Drop/Journey post
- submit verified proof
- join a Drop from content
- respond with proof

This measures participation, not empty consumption.

## KPI 2 — D7 Participating Retention
Activated new users who return on Day 7 and perform a meaningful participation action / eligible activated users.

## KPI 3 — Organic Participation Coefficient
New activated participants attributable to organic shares/invites divided by active participating senders in the period.

## Drivers
Acquisition: share→landing, landing→signup, signup→Join.  
Activation: time to interesting feed, Join, first Post, first feedback.  
Engagement: meaningful actions/session, Follow Journey, Watch Drop, response.  
Creator: first Drop, participants/Drop, repeat creator, creator→activation.  
Monetization: paywall, purchase, annual mix, subscriber retention.

## Guardrails
reports/1k impressions, blocks/1k, takedown rate, crash-free sessions, upload success, feed start latency, buffering, media cost/WMP, notification opt-out, refunds.
