# ProofMode — Launch Operations Runbook

## Daily during closed alpha
1. Review crash/error dashboard.
2. Review failed uploads/processing queue.
3. Review open safety reports; prioritize severe categories.
4. Review top/bottom feed posts and distribution anomalies.
5. Read 20–30 user sessions/comments qualitatively.
6. Check activation funnel and first-post time.
7. Check share → landing → join attribution.
8. Check media spend and bandwidth.
9. Curate one featured Drop and one comeback/fail story.
10. Log product decisions; avoid reacting to isolated anecdotes without evidence.

## Weekly
- cohort D1/D7 retention
- Weekly Meaningful Participants
- organic participation coefficient
- creator → activated participant conversion
- Drop health: % with 5+ active participants
- feed: watch depth + action-after-watch rate
- safety report rate / resolution SLA
- media cost per WMP
- Proof+ conversion / churn
- top acquisition sources

## Incident priority
### P0
Private-media exposure, auth bypass, payment/entitlement corruption, child-safety/severe illegal-content issue, destructive data loss.
Action: stop affected surface/distribution, preserve evidence/logs, invoke incident owner.

### P1
Widespread upload/feed outage, broken deep links, subscription purchase failures, moderation queue outage.
Action: degrade gracefully, post status/support notice, restore core loop.

### P2
Partial feature degradation, analytics gaps, cosmetic regressions.
Action: ticket and batch unless conversion/safety impact is material.

## Launch-day roles
- incident lead
- moderation lead
- creator/community lead
- analytics/growth lead
- engineering on-call
- social/editorial programmer

One person can hold several roles at small scale, but every role needs a named owner.

## Content programming
Morning: easy starter Drops + first-day courage.
Midday: funny/fail/chaos.
Evening: receipts, rank changes, comebacks, recap.

Do not feature dangerous stunts, humiliation, eating-disorder competition, substance-use pressure, sleep-deprivation competition, harassment, or other formats likely to turn “proof” into harmful escalation.
