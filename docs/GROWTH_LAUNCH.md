# ProofMode 3.0 — User Acquisition & Launch Playbook

## Launch principle
A social product cannot acquire demand into an empty room. Seed supply/culture before broad downloads.

## Exact acquisition order

### 1. House content
Create @proofmode, @proofmodefails, @proofmoderuns, @proofmodebuilds.
Seed Proof, Fail, Redemption, First Day, Streak, Rivalry, Beginner and Funny formats.

### 2. Closed communities
Run real groups: run clubs, gyms, colleges, builders, artists, friend groups.

### 3. Microcreator seed cohort
Target ~25–50 creators with participatory audiences, roughly 10K–500K followers as a starting discovery band—not a strict rule.

Offer:
- comped Creator
- Founder badge
- custom first Drop
- launch receipt kit
- feature eligibility

Do not pay large sponsorships before creator→activated-participant conversion is proven.

### 4. Build Black Founding 100 quietly
Private outreach only. Message:
“We are assembling the Founding 100 of a private creator network built around participatory communities.”

No public application.

### 5. Preload creator Drops
Each seed creator launches before broad public traffic.

### 6. Receipt Season 001
Primary campaign:
**DON’T POST THE PLAN. POST THE PROOF.**

Programming:
featured Drops, Receipt of the Day, Fail, Redemption, leader changes, creator battles, beginner picks.

### 7. Group-chat loop
Priority share experience:
**I did it. You’re next.**

Measure invite → view → accept → first post.

### 8. Social export
Native share sheet first to Instagram/TikTok/Snapchat/Messages/WhatsApp/Discord/etc.

### 9. Platform-specific integrations
Order:
1. universal/native sharing
2. Snapchat Creative Kit
3. TikTok Content Posting/draft after approval path
4. professional Instagram creator publishing where eligible
5. YouTube/Shorts only if creator demand justifies it

### 10. IRL
Automatic QR posters, story graphics, Drop cards and check-in assets for run clubs, gyms, campuses and events.

### 11. Paid acquisition
Only after organic content/retention works. Promote real creator/fail/comeback content with “Try It,” not generic corporate ads.

## Viral formats
- Fail → Redemption
- Rivalry
- Open #1 spot
- Beginner courage
- Proof Chain
- Crew rescue
- creator-vs-creator community battle

## Referral rewards
Avoid cash initially. Use low-cost software/status:
- recruiter count
- founder inviter badge
- cosmetics
- Crew unlocks
- seasonal progression

Never alter Proof Score/verification.

## Black acquisition
Source from founder intros, managers/agencies, member nomination and high-performing Creator users.

Internal CRM:
creator, manager, vertical, reach, engagement quality, audience fit, relationship owner, invitation, first Drop, attributable activations, retained participants.

Never expose qualification logic publicly.
