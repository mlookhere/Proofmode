# ProofMode 3.0 — Trust, Safety & Moderation Plan

UGC + challenges + fitness + comments + creator communities makes safety a launch system, not a later feature.

## Launch-required controls
- Terms acceptance before UGC
- Community Guidelines
- report post/comment/user/Drop
- block user
- mute/not interested
- moderation queue
- admin actions
- suspension/ban
- takedown
- action history
- support/contact
- basic age declaration

Apple currently requires UGC/social apps to provide filtering, reporting, blocking and published contact information. Google Play requires robust ongoing moderation with in-app reporting and blocking.

## Challenge policy

Allowed: ordinary exercise, learning, productivity, art, cooking, habits, sports, playful friend challenges.

Review/restrict: extreme endurance, unusual diets, high-risk sports, public confrontation, adult creator communities involving minors.

Prohibit: self-harm, starvation/extreme unsafe cuts, dangerous substances, illegal drug challenges, weapons, violence, dangerous driving, sleep deprivation, trespass/illegal acts, harassment/humiliation, sexual exploitation, dangerous challenge escalation.

## Age
Use declared age in MVP; do not position as a kids product. Add creator-content age/appropriateness controls. Review COPPA and relevant age-design laws before entering youth-heavy markets.

## Location
No precise location by default. Future coarse location is opt-in. Never expose live precise movement by default.

## Fail content
Allow ordinary failure/humor. Do not allow encouragement of dangerous behavior or targeted humiliation.

## “Call Cap”
Do not ship as a public crowd-downvote in MVP. If introduced later: friend/Crew context, anti-brigading, no automatic reputation penalty.

## Moderation flow
precheck → publish/hold → reports → priority queue → reviewer action → user notice → appeal/contact → repeat-offender enforcement.

Priority:
P0 child safety / credible threat / imminent self-harm
P1 illegal/dangerous
P2 hate/harassment
P3 scams/spam
P4 other community-rule issues

Creator verification means identity verification, not endorsement. Black members follow the same rules.
